I gave myself a September challenge and asked friends to join me. 30 minutes of exercise every day, plus one fun thing. Then I used AI to build an app to track it.

You log what you did, how long it took and how it felt. Everyone's month shows up as a grid on a shared board, so you can see who is keeping their streak and who is quietly slipping. You can add a photo of your fun thing and it builds into a gallery over the month.

There's a demo below if you want a look around. It gives you a throwaway account, no sign-up, and nothing you do affects anyone else.

https://september-tracker-demo.vercel.app/
